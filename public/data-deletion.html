Account & Data Deletion – Souqna

1) Self-service deletion (fastest)

Sign in at souqna.net, open Profile → Settings, and select Delete Account. This is available on both the website and the mobile app (Profile → My Account → Delete Account) and starts deletion immediately.

2) Email request option

If you cannot sign in, send a request from your registered email to payment@souqna.net, including the email or phone number your account was created with.

3) What gets deleted

  • Your account profile
  • Your listings and their photos
  • Your verification documents (ID photos, selfie), if you submitted seller verification
  • Your messages (where possible and technically feasible)

4) Processing time

Self-service and email requests are both completed within 30 days.

5) Legal retention notice

Some records may be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, tax, security, or dispute-resolution purposes as required by law.
