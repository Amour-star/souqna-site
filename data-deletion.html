Account & Data Deletion – Souqna

1) In-app deletion

Open the app and follow: Settings → Delete Account.

2) Email request option

If you cannot access the app, send a request from your registered email to support@souqna.net.

3) What gets deleted

  • Your account profile
  • Your listings
  • Your messages (where possible and technically feasible)

4) Processing time

Deletion requests are processed within 7–30 days.

5) Legal retention notice

Some records may be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, tax, security, or dispute-resolution purposes as required by law.
